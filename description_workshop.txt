[h1]Easy NPC Housing[/h1]

[h2]What is this mod?[/h2]
This mod lets you make simple NPC houses with the new "NPC Housing Wand" item. 
This mod was mainly meant for me to use and to get into terraria modding but I figured I would publish it and let others use it as well.
The item costs 50 wood which is around the amount of wood it would've costed to make the room by hand. It can be crafted at a work bench.

Currently, the houses have a perimeter of 5 (L) and 12 (H) because this is how I make them. But if this mod ever takes off and people want more customization I am open to that.
